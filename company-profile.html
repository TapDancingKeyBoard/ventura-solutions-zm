03 · Our Services

Share Registry & Capital Market Solutions

From register management to electronic voting, our full suite of services covers every aspect of share transfer agency and investor relations.

01

Share Register Management

We maintain accurate, centralised shareholder records for listed companies — managing shareholder detail updates, queries, trade activity monitoring, and customised periodic reporting to give you full visibility of your register at all times.

02

Bond Register Maintenance

We ensure accurate and secure recording and reconciliation of bondholder registers through liaison with LuSE, Brokers, and Custodians — with strict observation of interest and capital payment dates and quarterly reporting.

03

Corporate Action Handling

Our experienced team manages the full spectrum of corporate actions: dividend payment processing, AGM and EGM management, mandatory offers, share consolidations and bonus issuances, and Initial Public Offers (IPOs).

04

Medium Term Note Program (MTNP) Services

Full note management from pre-transaction through post-transaction — including advisory services, application processing, allotment, register management, and periodic reporting in line with LuSE CSD requirements.

05

Virtual Meeting Platform (VMP)

Our secure cloud-based VMP manages AGMs, EGMs, Board Meetings, and Webinars — with integrated shareholder validation, structured registration, document uploading, attendee interactions, and real-time voting in hybrid or physical formats.

06

Electronic Voting Services

Available to listed companies, private organisations, and cooperative societies. Our platform delivers real-time, auditable electronic voting with weighted and non-weighted options, full analytics, and results reporting accessible by administrators.

07

Unclaimed Dividend Management

We manage the identification, tracing, and processing of unclaimed dividends — covering contact tracing, deceased estate administration, periodic unclaimed dividend reports, and claimed dividend processing.

08

Dividend Reinvestment Plans & ESOP

We administer Dividend Reinvestment Plans (DRP) and Employee Share Ownership Plans (ESOP) — managing scheme registers, reconciliations, broker interfaces, and comprehensive post-reinvestment reporting.

04 · Special Program

Ventura Asset Reunification Program (VARP)

Did you invest in shares listed on the Lusaka Securities Exchange before 1994? You may have unclaimed assets waiting for you. VARP was created to reconnect investors and their beneficiaries with long-lost shares and dividends.

Are You a Lost Shareholder?

If you or a family member invested in shares listed on the Lusaka Securities Exchange (LuSE) before 1994, you may have unclaimed assets. VARP helps reunite dormant and inactive shareholders with their long-lost investments.

Deceased Estate Administration

We administer deceased shareholders' estates and handle share splits for beneficiaries, ensuring assets reach the rightful heirs legally and accurately.

Unclaimed Dividends Recovery

We identify and recover dividends that have gone unclaimed due to outdated banking details or unreported deaths — returning funds to their rightful owners.

Share Certificate Dematerialisation

We convert physical share certificates into secure electronic form through the LuSE Central Securities Depository (CSD).

05 · Investor Transaction Services

Equity Acquisition & Disposal Facilitation Services

We provide equity acquisition and disposal facilitation services designed to assist retail investors in buying and selling shares and other investment holdings efficiently and transparently within the Zambian capital market.

As a Share Transfer Agent and transaction facilitation service provider, we support investors through the administrative and coordination aspects of equity transactions, working in collaboration with licensed stock brokers and other regulated market participants where required.

Our Services Include:

  • Facilitation of equity acquisitions and disposals
  • Share transfer processing and shareholder record support
  • Investor onboarding and documentation assistance
  • Coordination with licensed stock brokers for trade execution
  • Transaction administration and settlement support
  • Shareholder services and registry support
  • Equity realisation support for retail investors
  • Assistance with portfolio restructuring and ownership transfers

While we facilitate and coordinate transactions, all securities trading and execution are conducted through duly licensed stock brokers in accordance with the Securities Act and applicable market regulations in Zambia.

Our objective is to improve accessibility, efficiency, and investor participation in the capital markets by providing reliable and professional support services to retail investors and corporate clients.
