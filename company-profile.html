01 · Who we are

Quality registry services shouldn't be only for the biggest companies.

Ventura Solutions Zambia Limited is a full-service share transfer agent, built on modern digital platforms to meet every share transfer need in Zambia's capital market.

We exist to bring high-quality, affordable share registry management to listed companies of all sizes — small, medium and large. Too often, good transfer secretarial services have been out of reach for smaller companies. We're changing that.

Our team brings a long, proven track record in securities registry, investor services and corporate actions, and has taken part in many corporate action transactions across Zambia.

We sit between listed companies and their shareholders — handling everything from routine register updates to complex corporate actions — so our clients can focus on their core business.

04 · Ventura Asset Reunification Program (VARP)

Your family's forgotten shares, found and returned.

Many Zambians bought shares on the Lusaka Securities Exchange before 1994 and lost track of them — through a move, a changed bank account or a death in the family. VARP reconnects investors and their beneficiaries with long-lost shares and dividends.

Deceased estates

Administering a late shareholder's holdings, including share splits so each heir receives their portion.

Unclaimed dividends

Recovering dividends lost to outdated bank details, unreported deaths or administrative gaps.

Dematerialisation

Converting physical share certificates into secure electronic holdings in the LuSE CSD.
