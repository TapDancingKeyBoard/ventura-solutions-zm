Ventura Asset Reunification Program (VARP)

Your family's forgotten shares, found and returned.

Many Zambians bought shares on the Lusaka Securities Exchange before 1994 and lost track of them — through a move, a changed bank account or a death in the family. VARP reconnects them with what they own.

Tell us what you know

A name, an old certificate or a dividend letter is enough to start.

We sort the paperwork
