Ventura Asset Reunification Program

Are You a Lost Shareholder?

If you or a family member invested in shares listed on the Lusaka Securities Exchange (LuSE) before 1994, you may have unclaimed assets. VARP helps reunite dormant and inactive shareholders with their long-lost investments.

Deceased Estate Administration

We administer deceased shareholders' estates and handle share splits for beneficiaries, ensuring assets reach the rightful heirs legally and accurately.

Unclaimed Dividends Recovery

We identify and recover dividends that have gone unclaimed due to outdated banking details or unreported deaths — returning funds to their rightful owners.

Share Certificate Dematerialisation

We convert physical share certificates into secure electronic form through the LuSE Central Securities Depository (CSD).
