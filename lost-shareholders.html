What we can help with

Three ways assets get lost. Three ways we bring them back.

Deceased estates

We administer a late shareholder's holdings and split the shares between beneficiaries, so each heir legally receives their portion.

Unclaimed dividends

Dividends often go unpaid because of outdated bank details or an unreported death. We find them and get them paid to the right person.

Old paper certificates

We convert physical share certificates into secure electronic holdings in the LuSE Central Securities Depository (CSD).

Questions
Who is VARP for?

Anyone who bought shares listed on the Lusaka Securities Exchange before 1994 and lost track of them — and the families and beneficiaries of shareholders who have passed away.

I don't have a share certificate. Can I still check?

Yes. The shareholder's full name and ID details are usually enough to start a search. Old paperwork helps, but isn't required to begin.

The shareholder has passed away. What happens?

We administer the shares as part of the estate and, where there are several beneficiaries, split them so each heir receives their portion.

Do I have to come to the office?

No. You can start by phone, WhatsApp or email. You're welcome to visit us in Woodlands, Lusaka if you prefer.

How long does it take?

It depends on the case — especially for estates. After the first search we'll tell you what we found and give you a realistic timeline.
